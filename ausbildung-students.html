What is Ausbildung?

Ausbildung is a dual vocational training program in Germany that combines classroom education with practical work experience. You will work for a company while attending vocational school gaining both knowledge and real-world experience.

Duration

2–3.5 years depending on the profession and your qualifications.

Monthly Salary

Earn between €1,000 and €1,300 per month during your training.

Career Opportunities

High chances of full-time employment after completing your Ausbildung.

Affordable Education

Free or low-cost programs available for both EU and non-EU students.

Requirements

To apply for an Ausbildung program in Germany, you’ll typically need:

🎓

High School Diploma

Or an equivalent qualification recognized in Germany.

🗣️

German Language Skills

At least B1 level to follow classes and communicate at work.

🛂

Valid Documents

Passport and visa for study/work purposes.

💪

Motivation

Enthusiasm to learn and build your career in Germany.
