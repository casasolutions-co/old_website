2. Erhebung und Speicherung personenbezogener Daten / Collection of Personal Data

Wir verarbeiten personenbezogene Daten, die Sie uns über unsere Website, per E-Mail, Kontaktformular oder durch Terminbuchungen mitteilen (z. B. Name, E-Mail-Adresse, Telefonnummer). We process personal data you provide to us via our website, email, contact form, or appointment booking (e.g. name, email address, phone number).

3. Zweck der Datenverarbeitung / Purpose of Data Processing

  • To respond to inquiries and provide consulting services
  • To arrange and manage appointments via Calendly
  • To improve our online services and communication
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Rechtsgrundlage / Legal Basis

The processing of your data is based on Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures), Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest), or your consent under Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR.

5. Verwendung von Calendly / Use of Calendly

Zur Terminvereinbarung nutzen wir den Dienst Calendly (Calendly LLC, 271 17th St NW, Atlanta, GA 30363, USA). When you schedule a meeting, the data you provide (name, email, date/time) is transmitted to Calendly and processed according to their privacy policy. For more information, please visit https://calendly.com/privacy.

6. Kontaktformulare / Contact Forms

Wenn Sie uns über ein Formular auf der Website kontaktieren, werden Ihre Angaben aus dem Formular inklusive der dort angegebenen Kontaktdaten zwecks Bearbeitung der Anfrage und für den Fall von Anschlussfragen bei uns gespeichert. The data will not be shared without your consent.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience and enable certain features. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies. However, this may limit website functionality.

9. Ihre Rechte / Your Rights

  • Right to access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

10. Speicherdauer / Data Retention

Personal data will be stored only as long as necessary for the purposes mentioned or as required by legal retention periods.
